# `SE_WaterColumn` — Multibeam Water Column Polar Fan & Time-Series Analysis Toolkit

`SE_WaterColumn` (`code/SE/SE_WaterColumn.c`, `code/SE/SE_WaterColumn.h`) is the primary interactive X11 water column visualization, target picking, and bottom-detection diagnostic engine in [`swathed`](swathed.html). Authored by John E. Hughes Clarke (June 2005–2021) with contributions from Jonathan Beaudoin (compression, along-track stack filtering, `.tgt` target management) and Carlos Rubio (`c_wcl_TOOLBOX` target hunter and beam-pattern compensation), it renders 80-ping blocks of Kongsberg EM (`.watercol`, `.kmall`), Reson 7k, and Mesotech M3 water column datagrams (`j_EM_watercolumn`) alongside split-beam phase (`.WC_Phase1`/`.WC_Phase2`) and multi-detect (`.extra`) soundings.

---

## Architecture & X11 Widget Layout (`WaterColumn_widget`)

Launched by clicking the **`WC`** icon (`icon_window[16]`) when `-wc` is enabled, `make_WaterColumn_widget()` creates `"WaterColumn Analysis Toolkit"` with **3 stacked graphics windows**, **2 sliders**, and **14 control buttons**:

### 3 Stacked Graphics Windows
1. **Graphic 0 (`draw_WaterColumn` / `draw_WC2D`, `800×400` or `1000×400` px):**
   * **Polar Fan / Beam-Time View (`draw_WaterColumn`):** Renders the scan-converted water column image (`WCim_c[swath]` or zoomed `WCzoom[swath]`, generated by `j_EM_combine_watercolumns()`) in one of three projections (`WC_View`):
     * `PIE_DIAGRAM`: True polar across-track distance vs. depth fan, corrected for transducer draft, heave, and roll.
     * `BEAM_TIME` / `ANGLE_TIME`: Rectangular beam-index or pointing-angle vs. two-way travel time (TWTT) sample grid.
   * Overlays physical WC bottom detections (`show_PHYS_BEAMS`, green/blue/red crosses), bottom-tracker HD/extra soundings (`show_BTRK_BEAMS`, cyan/red crosses), minimum slant range $R_0$ (`show_FIRST_TRACK`, yellow crosses), BDI candidate peaks (`show_BDI`, blue crosses), PDI zero-crossing phase picks (`show_PDI`, red diamonds), split-beam phase snippet gates (`show_PHASE`), and georeferenced `.tgt` acoustic targets (`show_TARGETS`, yellow labeled diamonds).
   * **2D Along-Track Curtain (`draw_WC2D`):** When a `.wc2d` vertical profile file is loaded (`-wc2d`) and toggled (`2` key), displays the along-track ping-vs-depth echogram curtain (`WC2D_fit` / `WC2D_zoom`).
2. **Graphic 1 (`draw_WC_TimeSeries`, `800×100` px — Along-Beam Range Trace):**
   * Plots the amplitude time series (`-64` to `+8` dB) along `selected_beam` from sample `0` to `MAX_length_WC_TimeSeries`, marking the real-time bottom pick (`GREEN` cross), `selected_WC_TWTT` (`RED` vertical cursor), and optional split-beam phase (`WHOLE_ping_WCP`).
3. **Graphic 2 (`draw_LowerSeries` / `draw_WC_AngleSeries` / `draw_WC_SwathSeries`, `800×100` px — Across-Beam Constant-TWTT Slice):**
   * Plots amplitude (dB) across all receive beam pointing angles (`-90°` to `+90°`) at the fixed range sample `selected_WC_TWTT`, color-coded by transmit sector (`Tx_sector_no`), or switches to along-track swath series (`show_SWATH_SERIES`).

| Control | Label | Function / Backing Variable |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Interactive Target Picking & Zoom Controls

* **Left-drag in Graphic 0:** Rubber-bands a spatial zoom window and re-renders full-resolution polar pixels via `process_WaterColumn_Zoom()`; pressing `r` resets to full swath view.
* **Middle-click in Graphic 0:** Converts clicked $(x, z)$ coordinates via `find_beam_and_twtt()` to select the exact `selected_beam` and `selected_WC_TWTT` sample, updating Graphic 1, Graphic 2, `SE_BDI_DP`, and `SE_PDI_LA`.
* **Target Management (`WC_add_target`, `WC_remove_target`, `WC_jump_to_next_target`):** Pressing `a` adds a georeferenced `OMG_Target` at `(selected_swath, selected_beam, selected_WC_TWTT)` and writes `<line>.tgt`; `d` deletes the nearest target; `n` jumps to the next target along the survey line; `<`/`>` tracks a moving target across adjacent pings (`WC_move_target_in_time()`).
